Who I Am

A Voice Shaped by
Passion & Craft

Muhammed Ihsan K P is a professional singer whose artistry blends classical training with contemporary studio expertise. Born with a natural affinity for melody, he honed his talents through formal education and real-world performance experience.

His work spans from intimate studio sessions to live stage performances with the celebrated Binsi & Imam Music Troupe — always carrying a signature warmth that connects deeply with audiences.

With a commitment to quality sound and genuine artistic expression, Ihsan continues to grow as a multifaceted music professional — singer, engineer, and composer in one.
